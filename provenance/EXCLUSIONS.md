# Export policy

Only selected source/text documentation, authored synthetic relation metadata,
and V111–V113 textual evidence are included. Original local files were not edited,
moved, compressed or deleted. No binary checkpoints or corpora were exported.
No final holdout/evaluation registry access, new training, cloud compute or
automation restart was performed for this publication task.

Historical workstation paths/private email are normalized; git metadata, caches,
credentials, notebooks with outputs, PDFs, archives and raw tensors are omitted.
This snapshot therefore cannot replace the local full-state recovery archive.
